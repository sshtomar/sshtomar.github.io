The Disease of Obsession

December 26, 2024 · 2 min read

Obsession is the most common disease among the greats. It often looks like passion, but it's not. Passion has balance. Obsession is unrelenting—a force that consumes time, relationships, and health.

The greats don't choose obsession. It chooses them. It whispers promises of greatness, mastery, legacy. And they listen because the alternative—ordinary—feels unbearable.

Yet obsession extracts its toll. It leaves behind abandoned friendships, neglected health, forgotten moments. The greats often look back and wonder: Was it worth it?

The answer is rarely simple. Obsession builds empires but burns bridges. It creates masterpieces but destroys the mundane joys that make life livable.

Perhaps the question isn't whether obsession is good or bad. Perhaps it's whether we can recognize its grip—and occasionally, just occasionally, loosen it.
