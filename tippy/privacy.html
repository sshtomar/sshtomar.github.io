Privacy Policy

Effective: February 15, 2026

Tippy ("the App") is developed by Shubham Tomar. This policy describes how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

Tippy collects the minimum information necessary to provide tip recommendations:

  • Approximate location — Used to tailor tip recommendations to regional customs. Your location is sent to our AI service to generate recommendations and is not stored.
  • Camera images — When you use receipt scanning, your camera captures an image of the receipt. The image is processed to extract the bill total and is immediately discarded. It is not stored on our servers.
  • Bill amounts and service selections — Used to calculate your tip recommendation. This data stays on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any account information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties

Third-Party Services

Tippy uses the Anthropic API (Claude) to generate AI-powered tip recommendations. When you request a recommendation, your bill amount, service type, context tags, and approximate location are sent to Anthropic's servers. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.

In-App Purchases

Tippy Pro subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Storage

Usage counts (for the free tier limit) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. No personal data is transmitted to or stored on external servers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact
